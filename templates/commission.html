Before you begin

How the atelier handles your details

Dossiers are read only by the master of the workshop and the patron's assigned engraver. Names are never published, and the Ephemeris Ledger records a piece by reference and city alone.

What happens after you lodge a dossier

The atelier replies within ten working days with questions about the site and a written quotation. Work begins on receipt of a deposit of one third.

Can the latitude be changed later?

Until the tympan is plotted, yes. Once the plate is cut a new latitude needs a new plate, which is why additional tympans exist.
