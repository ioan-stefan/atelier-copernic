Every line is calculated before it is cut.

  1. Survey

    We fix the owner's latitude to the arc-minute, usually for the house where the instrument will live.

    INPUT: φ, λ, ε (J2000)

  2. Project

    Almucantars and azimuths are projected stereographically, then plotted at twice scale before transfer to the plate.

    OUTPUT: TYMPAN GEOMETRY

  3. Solve

    Each train is searched tooth by tooth against the period it keeps. The Meridiana moon disc runs one day out in {{.LunarDrift}}.

    MAX WHEEL: 144 TEETH

  4. Chase

    Rete and limb are pierced and hand-chased, then lapped flat to within two microns.

    TOLERANCE: ±2 μm
